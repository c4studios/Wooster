## Scope and mode

The Wooster Core storefront home page (`app/page.tsx`), plus the cart drawer, /cart and /checkout/success in the same world. Mode: **Persuade**.

## Audience, job, action

- **Riders.** They decide between the handle (A$149), the Woo Mount add-on (A$49, black or orange), the Ultimate Bundle (A$179) and the Standalone Woo Mount (A$59), then add to cart.
- **C4 showcase visitors.** They judge the craft from a single captured frame of the first viewport.

## Proof and content

- The live WebGL print of the real parts.
- The four real product photos: the top-down tray, the open box, the parts on white and the closed boxes.
- The catalogue and the spec claims in the repo, approved by Caleb on 2 Oct 2026 and corrected to the photos: 4 bolts and 4 washers, with the washers stamped 316.
- No testimonials. The rider story ships with visible [PLACEHOLDER] markers.

## Chosen direction

**The Box and its Foam.** It was candidate #1 of 7 and IMPECCABLE'S PICK, and Caleb chose it on 2 Oct 2026. Seed key: 02f7b57c.

- The page is the Wooster Core box, opened.
- The hero is the lid, with the box's own layout:
  - WOOSTER CORE and PERFORMANCE BIG AIR across the top;
  - the line drawing in the middle, which here is the live wireframe print;
  - the contents and price list bottom-left;
  - ENGINEERED BY ARTY DESIGN bottom-right.
- Below the lid sits the kraft tray, with the real photo of every part in its foam cut-out. A packing list beside it spotlights parts and adds products to the cart.

Caleb's binding note: **keep the 3D printing design, it is the crux.** That means the white wireframe, the layer-by-layer clip, the orange hot layer and flash, and the part callouts. Build on them; do not replace them.

## Memorable moment

The lid's line drawing is printing itself. You can watch the orange layer trace the handle's top bar closed, and then point at any part to read what it is.
